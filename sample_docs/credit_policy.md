# Credit Decisioning Policy (sample)

This is a fictional policy used to demo DocQA Agent.

## Automatic approval
Applications with a credit score of 700 or higher and a debt-to-income ratio below 40% are approved automatically.

## Manual review
Scores between 620 and 699, or a debt-to-income ratio between 40% and 50%, go to manual review by a credit analyst. Analysts must record a reason for every decision.

## Decline
Applications with a score below 620 or a debt-to-income ratio above 50% are declined. Declined applicants receive an adverse action notice within 30 days.

## Documents required
Proof of income (two recent pay slips or a bank statement), a government-issued ID, and proof of address dated within the last 90 days.
