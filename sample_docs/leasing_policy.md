# Vehicle Leasing Policy (sample)

This is a fictional policy used to demo DocQA Agent.

## Lease terms
Standard lease terms are 24, 36 or 48 months. The default mileage allowance is 12,000 miles per year. Excess mileage is charged at $0.20 per mile at the end of the lease.

## Early termination
A lessee may end the lease early after the first 12 months. The early termination fee equals three monthly payments plus any past-due amounts. Before month 12, early termination requires approval from a credit manager.

## Maintenance and insurance
The lessee is responsible for scheduled maintenance and must keep comprehensive and collision insurance for the full term, with the lessor listed as additional insured.

## End of lease
At the end of the term the lessee can return the vehicle, buy it at the residual value stated in the contract, or roll into a new lease. Returned vehicles are inspected within 5 business days.
